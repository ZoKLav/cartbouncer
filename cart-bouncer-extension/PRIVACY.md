# Privacy policy — Cart Bouncer

Cart Bouncer runs entirely in Chrome. It does not collect, sell, transmit, or share browsing history, personal information, or usage data with its developer or third parties.

Blocklists, preferences, and filter rules are stored locally in Chrome using `chrome.storage.local`. They are used to filter Amazon search results in your browser. The extension reads product titles, product star ratings, available store/seller details, and links on supported Amazon pages. If you enable seller lookups, Cart Bouncer makes additional requests directly to the same Amazon domain you are browsing; these requests are needed only to discover merchant identity when Amazon does not display it in search results. Seller-lookups may send Amazon cookies to Amazon as part of those requests. No external analytics, advertising, or remote server is used by this extension.

The toolbar popup uses `activeTab` to read filtering counts from the active Amazon page and request a recheck. This information is not collected remotely. You can disable the extension's filtering and seller lookups in settings, or uninstall the extension to stop it from operating.

Cart Bouncer is independent of Amazon and not affiliated with Amazon. This policy describes version 1.2.0, dated November 22, 2026.
